## 2. THE 5-QUESTION CFO DIAGNOSTIC INTERVIEW

*Before an upload link is ever issued, the CFO or VP Finance must answer these 5 questions during a 15-minute qualification call. If they fail question #2 or #4, we do not proceed.*

| # | Diagnostic Question | What We Are Listening For (The Reality Check) | Pass / Red Flag |
|:---:|:---|:---|:---:|
| **1** | *What currently happens when your AP team suspects an overcharge?* | Is there an existing inspection habit, or does AP simply approve whatever carrier portals generate to avoid freight holds? | **PASS** if they have a short-pay or deduction habit; **RED FLAG** if they admit they rubber-stamp everything. |
| **2** | *Who actually owns the vendor dispute?* | A single, clear owner (AP Supervisor, Logistics Controller, or Treasury). | **CRITICAL FAIL** if the answer is "everyone" (organizational quicksand where disputes die). |
| **3** | *What happens to disputed freight invoices today?* | Are they held in an exception queue, short-paid on the remittance, or paid in full while hoping for a credit note? | Clarifies whether we are replacing an existing workflow or building one from scratch. |
| **4** | *What documents can your team produce within 48 hours?* | Centralized rate cards, BOLs, gate receipts, and past paid PDFs. | **CRITICAL FAIL** if extracting documents requires physical trips to remote warehouse archives. |
| **5** | *Have you successfully recovered money from ocean carriers before?* | Identifies what carrier evidentiary standards they have successfully negotiated in the past. | **GOLD MINE** if yes—reveals the carrier's exact historical settlement behavior and credit turnaround timeline. |

## 4. CONTRACTUAL DEFINITIONS & DATA RETENTION BOUNDARIES

### Unambiguous Legal Definition of "Recovered Value":
> **"Recovered Value"** is defined strictly and exclusively as:
> 1. **Direct Cash Refund**: A wire or check deposited into the Client's operating bank account from a carrier referencing a disputed invoice.
> 2. **Applied Credit Memo**: An official carrier Credit Memo that has been **actively applied and verified as a deduction against an existing, approved payable** in the Client's accounting ledger.
> 
> *Explicit Non-Billable Exclusions*:
> - Merely identifying an overcharge without carrier settlement is **not billable**.
> - An unapplied or expired credit memo is **not billable** until consumed against a real payable.
> - A disputed balance that the client voluntarily waives for commercial relationship reasons is **not billable**.

### Operationally Honest Data Retention Clause:
> *"Apex Recover contractually covenants that Client data shall **never** be used to train, fine-tune, or evaluate public or foundational machine learning models. Uploaded invoices, contracts, and evidence artifacts are retained in an isolated single-tenant cryptographic vault **solely for the active duration of the audit and dispute resolution lifecycle**. Upon written request following audit closure, Apex Recover will execute a complete cryptographic purge and issue an official Certificate of Destruction."*
