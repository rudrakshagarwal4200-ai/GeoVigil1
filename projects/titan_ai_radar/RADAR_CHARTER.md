## 1. Intelligence Collection Vectors

The Titan Radar autonomously monitors and analyzes 5 core intelligence vectors across the global AI ecosystem:

1. **Y Combinator & Top Accelerator AI Batches**:
   - Scraping YC Directory, Techstars, and A16Z Speedrun for the latest B2B AI startups.
   - Deconstructing their one-line pitch hooks and target customer segments.
2. **Enterprise AI Unicorns & Public AI Giants**:
   - Deep analysis of Palantir (AIP), Harvey AI (Legal), Sierra AI (Bret Taylor's enterprise customer service), Decagon (enterprise support), Glean (enterprise search), Cognition (Devin), and Scale AI.
   - Uncovering how they structure $1M+ enterprise ACVs and navigate procurement walls.
3. **Open-Source Agent Repositories & Research Papers**:
   - Tracking top agentic frameworks, multi-agent coordination protocols, and benchmark datasets.
4. **B2B SaaS Pricing & Packaging Databases**:
   - Monitoring how enterprise AI companies transition from per-seat pricing to outcome-based and token-metered billing.
5. **Venture Capital Investment Flows**:
   - Parsing Crunchbase, PitchBook, and SEC Form D filings to see where top institutional capital (Sequoia, Benchmark, Founders Fund, Lightspeed) is pouring billions into B2B AI.
